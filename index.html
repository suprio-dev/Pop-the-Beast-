🎮 About This Game

Pop the Beast! is a fast-paced arcade game where you test your reflexes by catching mischievous creatures appearing across a 3 × 3 grid. Score points, avoid dangerous beasts, and challenge yourself to beat your personal best!

📖 How to Play

🟢
Catch Normal Creatures

Click normal creatures to increase your score.

💀
Avoid Dangerous Beasts

Clicking a dangerous beast costs you one life.

❤
Protect Your Lives

Keep your remaining lives safe. Lose them all and the game ends.

🏆
Beat Your High Score

Your best score is saved in your browser using localStorage.

🎯 Choose Your Challenge

⏱ Time Attack

Score as many points as possible before the 30-second timer runs out.

♾ Endless Mode

There is no countdown. Keep playing until you lose all your lives.

⚡ Quick reflexes. Careful clicks. Happy hunting!
